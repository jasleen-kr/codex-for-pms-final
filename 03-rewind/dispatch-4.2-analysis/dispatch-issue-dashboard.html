5 · Why aggregate recovery can hide continued harm

Final week: the four receive 3/165 pings and take none; other responders take 120/162. Aggregate acceptance is 120/165 (72.7%). Post-release group acceptance estimates have a small denominator: only 25 pings to the four over three complete weeks.

Data, definitions and reproducibility
Sources: rook.pings, rook.callouts, rook.responders and rook.support_tickets; routing repository; September interviews. All pings and ticket records were previously retrieved and reconciled. Acceptance = taken / all pings. Missed = no answer before timeout; not proof of delivery failure. Usual area does not establish per-callout eligibility. Rates are weighted by pings. Main before/after comparison uses 6 and 3 complete weeks. Mixed week remains in graphs. Exact-date comparison excludes 12 August itself; the two analyses intentionally have different denominators. No historical availability, ranking scores, travel-time snapshots or device timing fields are present in these tables. Earlier incidents existed. Observational timing cannot establish causation.
