Panel 10 · No premature verdict

CASE STILL OPEN

WHAT THE EVIDENCE ESTABLISHESAcceptance falls, misses increase, allocation becomes uneven, and specific ticket counts match the ping records.
WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISHWhich release change caused which responder’s decline, whether every offer reached a device, or whether Farlight’s particular offers went to The Gale.
