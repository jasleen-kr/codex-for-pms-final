Agreement and its limits

ComparisonEvidence
Count agreementAll 15 fully covered explicit seven-day claims match the ping records.
Post-release themes32 quiet reports + 14 disappearing reports − 2 overlaps = 44 distinct tickets, out of 105 after release day.
Scope of reportingQuiet reports: four handlers. Disappearing reports after release day: ten handlers. Repeated reports are not independent customers or incidents.
Notification deliverySent pings and missed outcomes do not establish delivery, display time, response time or withdrawal time.
CausationTiming is consistent with release effects. Routing, availability and device-event logs are needed to establish the cause.
Aggregate recoveryAcceptance reaches 72.7% in the final full week, still below every complete pre-release week; quiet reports continue.
Other ticketsFilters, sign-in, equipment and other topics contribute to total ticket volume; ping records cannot validate those complaints.
Source coverage and definitions
Rook database: 147 support tickets, 29 June–7 September; 1,696 pings, 29 June–6 September. Earlier June and later September ping data are unavailable. Weekly rates use total taken or missed divided by all pings. Database timestamps are treated as local time, without timezone conversion. Zero tickets is distinct from unavailable ping data. Quiet and disappearing categories follow the documented subject rules in ticket-ping-audit.json. Earlier related signal/device tickets are shown separately. Theme absence does not establish symptom absence.
