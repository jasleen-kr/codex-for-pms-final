Reasons and analysis · evidence versus inference

FactorWhat this comparison showsWhat we can conclude
Local demandDemand falls in both usual areas, while the responders’ pings move in opposite directions.These local demand changes alone do not explain the divergence.
Shorter answer window4.2 cuts 90 seconds to 60. Farlight’s release-week missed share is 40%; The Gale’s is 20%.Timeout is a plausible contributor. Delivery and response timing are needed to establish why they miss offers.
Acceptance-history feedbackCode subtracts 0.12 for a miss or decline, adds 0.08 for acceptance, and has no time-based recovery. After the mixed week, Farlight takes 0/4; The Gale takes 42/58.Farlight has few chances to rebuild score; Gale has many accepted offers. Actual scores and ranks are not available.
Proximity reweighting4.2 raises proximity weight 0.45 → 0.60 and lowers history weight 0.40 → 0.25.Ranking could change independently of misses. We cannot infer that The Gale was closer to callouts.
Availability and eligibilityLinda reports Farlight’s availability and tags appear correct. We have no historical candidate lists.Her account may look available without proving eligibility for any particular incident.
Direct transfer of workFarlight’s offers fall and The Gale’s rise; they usually cover different areas.We cannot say that Farlight’s particular offers were reassigned to The Gale.
