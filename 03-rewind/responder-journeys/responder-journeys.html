16 responders · 1,696 actual pings · 29 June-6 September 2026. Bars show offers, not confirmed phone delivery.

The blue score line is a reconstruction, not an observed score. It starts everyone at 0.50 on 29 June, applies +0.08 for accepting and -0.12 for declining or missing, and limits scores to 0-1. Starting scores, actual rankings, historical availability and outcome times are absent. Events are replayed in sent-time order; the score change is drawn at send time as an approximation. No waiting-time recovery is added. This is the history component only, not the complete job-specific ranking score.

Volume comparison: six full weeks, 29 June-9 August, versus three full weeks, 17 August-6 September. The mixed week of 10-16 August is shown but excluded from the before/after averages. The vertical line marks 12 August; grey bars mark the mixed week.

How to read this: Green = accepted, gold = declined, pink = missed. Hover over bars or the blue line for numbers. Expand the tables for exact weekly counts and every recorded offer.

Sources: Rook database responders and pings; saved all-pings.csv; dispatch-routing/history.py, offer.py and config.py. Coverage was checked against the database.
