After a missed ping

The offer expires at 60 seconds. Dispatch offers the callout to the next eligible responder and applies a 0.12 history deduction, down to a floor of 0.00. Check notification delivery and stated availability with your handler. Each future callout gets a fresh ranking using availability, capability, travel time and history. Accepting a future offer adds 0.08 to history.

Let old outcomes fade

Move everyone’s history halfway toward 0.50 over 14 days without a new outcome. At 0.00, the example becomes 0.25. New outcomes still affect the score.

Account for delivery

Try a smaller 0.04 deduction for a displayed offer that expires and zero deduction when display is unconfirmed. An explicit decline costs 0.12.

Offer a recovery opportunity

Try one first offer for the selected hero in a 10-callout example, with the same capability and travel-time fit. If it expires, Dispatch moves on at 60 seconds. Measure the effect on assignment speed.

These three rules are proposals for a pilot. The offer deadline is 60 seconds from send in every scenario. The history values start from the saved replay model and change as you try outcomes here.
